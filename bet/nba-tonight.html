Prices are the cost to win $1: 48¢ means you pay 48¢ and get $1 back if it hits (+108 in sportsbook terms). Kalshi prices include its trading fee; DraftKings' is its American price converted. Gap = how far apart the cheapest and dearest source are on the same bet: free money the books and exchanges leave on the table. Market = the average of every source's fair price; Ours = our model alone; Value = expected return per $1 at the cheapest price, using 35% ours / 65% market. Faded = thin market (wide bid/ask, little traded) or too little data on the player; long shot = under 15¢ or over 85¢, where every model's tails are least reliable.
