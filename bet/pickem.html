The pick'em board

PrizePicks, Underdog, Pick6 and Betr post a line and pay a fixed multiplier for the whole entry. So no pick has a price of its own. A pick is worth playing when our chance beats the break-even of the entry you put it in.

Our proj = our expected line for this game. Books imply = what the sportsbooks' prices for the same prop point to; when they post one, Chance blends the two, the same way the prop board does. Without a sportsbook price the chance is our model alone (marked model only), so trust it less. check line = far from the sportsbooks' number or over 72%: usually a goblin or demon line that pays a different multiplier. Those stay out of built entries. vs B/E = chance minus the lowest break-even this app offers. Other apps = the same prop's line elsewhere; a half point is a lot here.
