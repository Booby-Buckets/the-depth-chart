How to read it. Once books post, every box shows the best price across every sportsbook and which book has it; a green box clears our expected-value bar (the EV is printed in it). Before books post, every box is our number: the spread with our win chance under it, our projected total, and the fair moneyline (no vig). Our favourite's boxes are blue. Once a book posts, its number is big and ours sits underneath; a green box is the side our model likes by a real margin (1.5+ points on spreads, 2+ on totals, 3+ points of win chance on moneylines).
