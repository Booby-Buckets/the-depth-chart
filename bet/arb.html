Middles

—
A middle takes one side at one number and the other side at a different number at another place. Land between them and both win; otherwise one wins and one loses. Both win is the chance of landing in the gap, from a normal curve around the market's own line (integer scores, so pushes count). Worst = the worst result per $100 staked. no-lose = even the worst result is a profit.

—
Before you bet both sides: prices move, so check both are still there and place the bigger or slower one first. Books cap stakes and limit accounts that arb a lot. A price far off every other book can be voided as an obvious error, so anything returning over 5% is tagged check price. Exchange prices already have fees taken out, but a thin exchange may not fill your whole stake. Check the books grade the market the same way (overtime counts in college spreads, totals and moneylines at the big US books). Pinnacle isn't included because Americans can't bet there, and DFS apps pay fixed multipliers, so they can't be arbed.
