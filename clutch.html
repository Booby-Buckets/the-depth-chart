Clutch Role Shift

When the game is on the line, who wants the ball — and who still makes it? Pick a team, then a player, to see how his shot-making load and his efficiency move in the clutch: the last five minutes of a one-possession game, against the rest of his shots.
Clutch = the last 5:00 of the second half or overtime with the score within five. From each shot's game clock and running margin. Shot load is the player's share of his team's field-goal attempts (does the offense run through him late?); efficiency is eFG%. Clutch samples are small by nature — a season yields a few dozen such shots — so read these as tendencies, and the card flags thin samples. Use the season toggle to pool all seasons for a bigger, steadier read, or isolate one year. The shot chart plots every clutch attempt where it was taken; hover a marker for the game clock, the margin, and whether it fell.
