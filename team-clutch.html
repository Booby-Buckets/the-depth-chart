Team Clutch Identity

Who is this team when the game is on the line? Pick a team to see how its offense and defense move in the clutch — the last five minutes of a one-possession game — who it runs the offense through late, and where those shots come from. Pooled across every season with game-clock data, or one year at a time.
Clutch = the last 5:00 of the second half or overtime with the score within five, from each shot's game clock and running margin. Offense is the team's own eFG%; defense is opponent eFG% in the same games; net is the gap. Who closes is each player's share of the team's clutch field-goal attempts. Clutch samples are small by nature, so pool all seasons for a steadier read. These describe tendencies to plan around — not verdicts.
