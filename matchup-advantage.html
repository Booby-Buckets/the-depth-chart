Matchup Advantage

Which kind of opponent does a player feast on, and which one takes him out of the game? Pick a team, then a player, for his scoring efficiency against each defensive archetype — elite or porous, havoc or disciplined, uptempo or grind — distilled into one Matchup Advantage Index and a best/worst read you can plan around.
Efficiency is true shooting % (points per shooting possession, so it rewards free throws and threes). Each archetype is a lens on the opponent's 2025-26 identity (from team_dna) — the lenses overlap: one opponent can be both elite and havoc, so a game can count toward more than one bucket. Δ is versus the player's own 2025-26 baseline; buckets need 4+ games. The Matchup Advantage Index is the spread between his best and worst archetype — how much his efficiency swings with the style he's facing.
