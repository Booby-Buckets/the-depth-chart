How the Big Board score is built
Every prospect is graded 0–100 on four lenses, then blended into the Board score with an NBA-style, upside-led weighting (Translatability 30% · Upside 28% · Stock 24% · Readiness 18%).
Translatability — how the game projects to the next level: size for position, shooting touch (3P% + FT%), defensive playmaking, scoring efficiency (TS%), and ball security.
Readiness — how NBA-ready right now: current production and role, efficiency at volume, college experience, and polish.
Upside — ceiling: youth, physical/defensive tools, scouting-grade ceiling, and per-minute flashes.
Stock (Draftability) — the market read: scouting grade, production, translatable skill, and level of competition.
