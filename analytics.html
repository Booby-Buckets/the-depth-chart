Net Rating ★
ORtg minus DRtg per 100 possessions. The single best predictor of wins and losses. +8 = tournament team. +15 = national title contender. -5 = bubble. Conference-adjusted.
Power Rating
Composite score = Net Rating (50%) + TDC Team Grades — coach, depth, recruiting, NIL (30%) + Roster Grade Average (20%). Captures what stats don't.
ORtg
Offensive Rating — projected points per 100 possessions. Conference-adjusted. B10/SEC/B12 baseline = 1.0. Mid-major stats deflated to reflect competition level.
DRtg
Defensive Rating — projected points allowed per 100 possessions. Built from player grades (20%), block/steal/dreb box stats (50%), frontcourt height (20%), and foul discipline (10%). Lower is better.
Avg Grade / Top-8
Average TDC grade across all rostered players, and the average of the top 8 graded players specifically. These feed into the power rating and defensive projection.
eFG%
Effective Field Goal % — adjusts FG% for the added value of 3-pointers. Formula: (FGM + 0.5×3PM) / FGA.
Pace
Estimated possessions per 40 minutes. Higher = faster tempo.
TOV%
Turnover percentage — turnovers per 100 possessions. Lower is better.
3PAR / FTAR
3-Point Attempt Rate and Free Throw Attempt Rate — how perimeter-oriented and how aggressive a team is at drawing fouls.
Conf Str
Conference strength multiplier used to adjust stats. SEC/B10/Big12 = 1.00 (baseline). All ratings are scaled so teams across conferences are comparable.
Game Simulator
Simulate any matchup up to 10,000 times. Results include win probability, projected score, and key metric comparison. Note: This is a predictive model with built-in variance — not a guaranteed outcome.

How The Model Works
The TDC predictive model builds team ratings from individual player statistics, adjusted for conference strength. It is intentionally designed as a directional model — giving a reasonable prediction with honest uncertainty ranges, not a precise forecast.
Offensive Rating
Built from player-level stats aggregated to a 5-player team. Points per 100 possessions is calculated from scoring, shooting efficiency, turnover rate, and free throw generation. All offensive metrics are multiplied by a conference strength factor to normalize stats across conferences.
Defensive Projection
Because rosters turn over significantly in college basketball, last year's team defensive stats are poor predictors of next year's defense. Instead, TDC projects defense from individual defensive attributes of the current roster: block rate, steal rate, defensive rebounding, and foul tendency. This approach is better suited for offseason projections.
Conference Translation
A player averaging 20 PPG in the Sun Belt is not the same as one averaging 20 PPG in the SEC. Each conference has a strength multiplier (SEC/B10/Big12 = 1.0 baseline) that adjusts stats when comparing across conferences. These multipliers will be refined over time.
Game Simulator
The simulator runs N independent game simulations between two teams. Each simulation adds random variance (±${CONFIDENCE_RANGE} points) to reflect real game unpredictability. The win percentage reflects how often each team wins across all simulations — not a guarantee.
Limitations
This model does not account for injuries, home court advantage, travel, rest days, or coaching adjustments. It is based solely on statistical attributes of the current roster. Treat all outputs as educated estimates, not predictions.
