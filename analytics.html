TDC Analytics Engine V1

Team Analytics

Built from 2026-27 projected rosters. Ratings are computed using the ecosystem model: spacing score, creation score, defensive score, rebounding, rim pressure, turnover risk, and heliocentric penalty. Net Rating (ORtg − DRtg) is the primary ranking — the best single predictor of winning.

⚡ Champ. Equity
Championship probability score (0–100). Balances talent, offensive/defensive ceiling, synergy, experience and tournament translation. Primary ranking metric.
Talent Index
Grade-weighted score of top 8 players. Superstar bonus for 90+ grade players. Heliocentric elite creator adds extra points.
Ceiling / Floor
Ceiling = best realistic outcome (boosted by star power + synergy). Floor = collapse risk (penalized by variance). Neither replaces the main score.
Net Rating
ORtg minus DRtg. Best predictor of wins and losses. +8 = tournament team, +15 = title contender, negative = bubble.
ORtg / DRtg
Points scored / allowed per 100 possessions, derived from team ecosystem factors. Base = 108 offensive, 103 defensive.
Spacing
Weighted 3P% × √3PA across rotation. High = defense must respect perimeter, opens lanes for slashers and creators.
Creation
APG weight + FTA rate + 3PA rate − TOV rate. Values shot creation, downhill pressure and rim attacks over safe passing.
Defense
Rim protection (BLK), perimeter pressure (STL), length (avg height), rebounding and defensive rating combined.
TOV Risk
Weighted turnovers per game. High = offense prone to breakdowns. Each turnover costs ~0.8 ORtg points.
Helio ★
Team has a dominant high-usage creator. Applied −2 ORtg penalty but boosts championship equity — star power matters in March.
