Team Analytics

Built from 2026-27 projected rosters. Ratings are computed using the ecosystem model: spacing score, creation score, defensive score, rebounding, rim pressure, turnover risk, and heliocentric penalty. Net Rating (ORtg − DRtg) is the primary ranking — the best single predictor of winning.

Computing from projected rosters…
Net Rating
ORtg minus DRtg. Best predictor of wins and losses. +8 = tournament team, +15 = title contender, negative = bubble.
ORtg / DRtg
Points scored / allowed per 100 possessions, derived from team ecosystem factors. Base = 108 offensive, 103 defensive.
Spacing
Weighted 3P% × √3PA across rotation. High = defense must respect perimeter, opens lanes for slashers and creators.
Creation
APG × 2.2 + USG × 0.4 − TOV × 1.2. Measures offensive generation. High = team can break down defenses.
Defense
STL × 2.3 + BLK × 2.6 + RPG × 0.55. Aggregated defensive output across rotation players.
Rim Pressure
Weighted FTA per game. Teams that get to the line control tempo and score efficiently.
TOV Risk
Weighted turnovers per game. High = offense prone to breakdowns. Each turnover costs ~0.8 ORtg points.
Variance
Elevated by high-3PA reliance and freshman presence. High variance = wider range of outcomes game-to-game.
Helio ★
Team has a dominant high-usage creator (USG >26). Applied −2 ORtg penalty — heliocentrism is harder to sustain.
