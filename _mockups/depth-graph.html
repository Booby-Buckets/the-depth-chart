Depth chart as a graph: three options

All three use Duke's real projected 2026-27 depth chart. Pick one (or mix), and it replaces the position cards on every team page. Editing, drag-to-reorder and the player editor carry over.
Option AMinutes bars
Each position is a 40-minute bar split between the players who play it. Bar length = projected minutes, colour = overall. You see at a glance who carries each spot and how deep it is.
