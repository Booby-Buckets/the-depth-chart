On/Off & WOWY

How a team scores and defends with each player on the court vs off it, reconstructed from NCAA play-by-play lineups. The headline number is Impact — how many points per 100 possessions the team is better with a player on the floor versus on the bench.

Impact — the one number to start with: how many points per 100 possessions the team is better with this player on the floor vs. on the bench. Bigger is better.

On Net / Off Net — the team's net rating (points scored minus allowed, per 100) with the player On the court and Off it. Impact is simply On Net − Off Net.
On / Off ORtg — points the team scores per 100 possessions. DRtg — points it allows per 100 (lower is better defense).
These are raw on/off — not yet adjusted for opponent or lineup quality, so a player with few bench minutes can show a noisy Off number. Read it as context, not proof.
