On/Off & WOWY

How a team scores and defends with each player on the court vs off it, reconstructed from NCAA play-by-play lineups. The headline Impact is the on/off swing adjusted for sample size — shrunk toward zero when it rests on few bench-unit possessions, then ranked against every qualified player — so the leaderboard reflects real impact, not small-sample noise. The unadjusted Raw number is shown alongside.

Impact — the one number to start with: the on/off swing (per 100 possessions) adjusted for sample size and ranked vs the field. Tier comes from the vs Field percentile.

Raw — the unadjusted swing (On Net − Off Net). A qualifying player needs 300+ on and 200+ off possessions; below that, Impact shows “—” because the sample is too thin to trust.
On Net / Off Net — the team's net rating (points scored minus allowed, per 100) with the player On the court and Off it. Raw impact is On Net − Off Net.
On / Off ORtg — points the team scores per 100 possessions. DRtg — points it allows per 100 (lower is better defense).
On/off is measured relative to a player's own teammates and isn't adjusted for opponent — a good player on a weak team can post a big swing. Read it as context, not proof.
