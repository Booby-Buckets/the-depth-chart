On/Off & WOWY

How a team scores and defends with each player on the court vs off it, reconstructed from NCAA play-by-play lineups. On/Off Net is the swing in net efficiency (points per 100 possessions) when a player is on the floor versus on the bench. Positive = the team is better with him out there. Sample size and role still matter — read it as context, not proof.
