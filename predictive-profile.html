Predictive Profile

The whole book on a player, on one card. Pick a team, then a player, for a scout's-eye summary that pulls his predictive identifiers together — how he responds to pace and to pressure, which opponents he feasts on, and whether he closes or fades — into the read you'd give before tip-off.
Every identifier is cut from the player's own 2025-26 record: box logs classified by the opponent's tempo and forced-turnover rate (team_dna tertiles) for pace and pressure, opponent archetypes for matchup, his shot log by half for the closing read, and his team's on/off splits (sample-shrunk, ranked vs the field) for the lineup amplifier. Small-sample buckets (under 4 games / thin shot volume) are marked as such and left out of the summary. These describe tendencies to plan around — not verdicts on a player.
