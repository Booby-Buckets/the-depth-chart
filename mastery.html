Who has the green light?

Rim shots, free throws and catch-and-shoot threes are the efficient shots. Anything else is a mastery shot: a player earns the green light on it once he shows he makes it at 1.1 points per shot or better, which is 55% on a two or 36.7% on a three. The rule is Saint Louis coach Josh Schertz's.
