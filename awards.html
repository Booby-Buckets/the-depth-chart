Projected Awards

Projected 2026-27 honors: three All-America teams nationally, and three All-Conference, two All-Defensive and two All-Freshman teams in each conference, five players per team.
Building projections…
How selections are made: every rostered D1 player is scored with the same deterministic model — projected BPM (last season's Box Plus/Minus run through the measured year-over-year lag model, with class-progression bumps), TDC grade, and projected production. Players marked out for the season are excluded. The top five in each pool make the 1st team, the next five the 2nd, and so on — so a conference can never have more than five players on any one team.
