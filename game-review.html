Automatic Game Review

Pick a team, pick a game — and get the four-factor autopsy: what actually decided it. Every result in the database is broken down into shooting, turnovers, the glass, the line, three-point variance and shot quality, ranked by how many points each swung.
Reads the box score for the selected game (four factors: effective FG%, turnovers, offensive-rebound %, free-throw rate), plus three-point variance vs the ~34.5% D-I average and — where shot-by-shot data exists — shot quality (expected eFG from where the shots came vs what actually went in). Point impacts are estimates from standard four-factor weighting, ranked to explain the final margin; the underlying box numbers are exact.
