Futures

Our % comes from 2,000 simulated seasons: every regular-season game, every conference tournament, the committee's bracket, then March. Fair odds is that chance as a price with no vig: bet it only when the book pays more. Edge = our % minus the book's % with the vig removed.

Conference odds

Reg. season = win or share the regular-season title; Outright = win it alone. Tournament = win the league's real conference tournament bracket and the automatic bid.
