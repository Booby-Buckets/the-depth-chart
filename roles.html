Pick a team above to read its personnel.
Role signals are each player's 2025-26 advanced rates: usage, assist %, three-point attempt rate, total-rebound %, block % and steal %, scaled to typical D-I ranges. The archetype is derived from those signals + position; it describes what a player did, not how to use him. Newcomers with no D-I season show once they have a track record.
