Roster Consistency

How reliable is each player night to night? Instead of averages, this reads the full distribution — each returner's ceiling, floor, volatility and predictability, plus how often they actually clear key lines (20+ points, double-digit boards) and how often they lay an egg. Transfers carry the history from wherever they played.
Pick a team above to break down its roster.
Consistency is measured from each player's 2025-26 game log (games with 5+ minutes). The volatility index is the coefficient of variation of their per-game output (spread ÷ average); the consistency score is 100 − volatility, so a steady 14-a-night scorer rates higher than a 4-or-24 scorer with the same average. Floor/ceiling are the 20th and 80th percentile games. Roster score is minutes-weighted across players with a D-I game history.
