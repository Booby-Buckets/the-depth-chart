What this is

Every men's Division I play since 2008, searchable. Pick a season and nothing else and you get that season's standout plays across D-I: the biggest swings in win probability and the best shot-making. Pick a team and every play of its season loads live from ESPN (5 to 10 seconds), so you can filter down to a player, a shot type or a moment in the game.

Each result is one play: the play-by-play line, the game and date, the clock and score, and what the play was worth. Click a play to open that game's box score.

The numbers
WP added
How much the play changed the acting team's chance of winning, in percentage points. A +94.8% buzzer-beater took a team from about 5% to a win. Win probability comes from our live model: the score margin, the time left, which team has the ball, and a pregame edge from the two teams' power ratings plus home court.
xPts
Expected points: what the average D-I shot from that spot is worth (make rate for that zone × 2 or 3). A corner three is about 1.07, a mid-range jumper about 0.70, a shot at the rim about 1.28.
POE
Points over expected: points scored minus xPts. A made 40-footer is about +2.0; a missed layup at the rim is about −1.3. Summed over many shots it measures shot-making.
Shot distance
Feet from the basket, from ESPN's shot location.
Score margin
The acting team's lead after the play (negative when trailing).
The filters
Season
2007-08 to now. A season is named for the year it ends.
Team
Loads every play of that team's games. Leave it on Any team for the D-I standouts.
Side
With a team picked: its own plays, its opponents' plays, or both.
Player
Plays a player made or assisted on (players with at least 5 plays for the team).
Play type
Any shot, 3PT, 2PT (jumper, layup, dunk, tip-in), free throw, turnover, steal, block, rebound or foul.
Result
Made, missed, or assisted.
Period
1st half, 2nd half, or overtime.
Situation
Clutch: last 5:00 of the 2nd half or OT, within 5 points before the play. Final minute: last 1:00, within 3. Last 5 seconds: the final 5 seconds of the 2nd half or OT. Tie or go-ahead: a make that tied the game or took the lead.
Sort
Biggest WP added, most costly plays (WP added low), points over expected, longest shots, or game order.
Column filter
Any number against a value, for example WP added % ≥ 20 or Shot distance ≥ 30.
Text
Words in the play-by-play line or a player's name, for example alley or and one.

Coverage

Play-by-play runs from 2007-08 to now. For 2008 through 2013, ESPN has play-by-play for only about half of the games (mostly missing low-major matchups), so those seasons are partial. Shot locations exist for most games since 2014; a shot without one is valued at the D-I make rate for its type. Search shows the top 150 matches.
