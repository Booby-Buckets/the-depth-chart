Who makes their own, and who needs to be set up? Pick a team to see how much of each player's — and the roster's — scoring is self-created off the dribble versus assisted. The read that tells you whether taking away a passer, or a shooter's space, actually takes away the bucket.

A made field goal is "assisted" if the play-by-play credits an assist, "self-created" if not, from each player's 2025-26 shot log (at whichever school he played). The bar splits self-created vs assisted makes; the sub-line shows how much of his twos and threes were assisted (a low two-point assist rate = creates off the bounce; a high three-point assist rate = catch-and-shoot). Team % is the roster's combined makes.
