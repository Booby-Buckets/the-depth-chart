Game Breakdown

The full story of a single game. Pick a team, then a game, for how it actually unfolded — the momentum swing chart, the biggest runs, the stretch that decided it, who did the damage on both sides, and the half-by-half read. The film session, in numbers.
Reconstructed from the 2025-26 shot-by-shot timeline (game clock + running score) plus the box score. The momentum chart plots the margin from the selected team's side over the course of the game; runs are the biggest sustained margin swings; game rating is a 0–10 grade scaled from Hollinger's single-number box impact. Hover the momentum line to read the score at any moment. Free throws move the running score but aren't plotted as shots, so the line tracks the scoreboard, not shot attempts.
