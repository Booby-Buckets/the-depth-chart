Game Context

Does the game come to a player, or does the game change him? Pick a team, then a player, to see how his shooting, turnovers and fouls actually move when the tempo speeds up — and when he's hunted by a ball-pressure, havoc defense. The stuff a matchup plan is built on.
Splits are cut from the player's own 2025-26 game log (min 4 games a side). "Fast / slow" is the opponent's season tempo (top vs bottom third of D-I possessions); "havoc / low-pressure" is the opponent's defensive turnover rate (top vs bottom third of forced-TO%). Turnovers and fouls are per-40 so playing time doesn't skew them; eFG% counts threes at 1.5×.
