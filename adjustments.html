Built from the team's 2025-26 shot-by-shot timeline (game clock + running score). Halftime Adjustment = 2nd-half offensive eFG% minus 1st-half — do they come out of the break sharper, plus how the shot diet changes. Run Response = eFG% on shots taken right after the opponent swings the margin 6+ against them (from a recent peak, within ~2:30 of game clock), vs the team's season baseline — answering runs vs folding. Run states are rare, so that sample is small and read as a tendency; eFG counts threes at 1.5×.
