Does a team adapt when the game turns, or does it ride the tide? Pick a team to see how it responds on both ends after halftime — the one point where a staff adjusts after a full look at the opponent — how it holds up right after the other team runs, and whether it keeps its poise when trailing. Pooled across every season with game-clock data, or one year at a time.
Pick a team above to read its adjustment profile.
Built from the shot-by-shot timeline (game clock + running score) of every season with clock data. Halftime — Offense = 2nd-half team eFG% minus 1st. Halftime — Defense = opponent 2nd-half eFG% minus 1st, from the opponents' shots in the same games (a fall = the defense tightens). Run Response = eFG% right after the opponent swings the margin 6+ against them (from a recent peak, within ~2:30 of game clock), vs the team's baseline. Poise Under Deficit = eFG% while trailing 6+, vs baseline. Run and deficit states are rarer, so those samples are smaller and read as tendencies; pool all seasons for a steadier read. eFG counts threes at 1.5×.
