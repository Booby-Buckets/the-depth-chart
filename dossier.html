Player Dossier

The complete file on a player. His projected 2026-27 line, then the evidence a coach actually game-plans on — how his efficiency, turnovers and role move with pace, ball pressure, opponent archetype and the clutch, each shown with the real splits, not a single number. Where he scores from, and the confidence behind it all.
Projected line = the site's forward-looking projection (last season adjusted for role & development). Situational splits are cut from his own 2025-26 game and shot logs vs the opponent's tempo / forced-turnover rate / defensive archetype (from team_dna), and the clutch (last 5:00 within 5, from the shot clock). Confidence reflects sample size — clutch and archetype buckets are thin by nature and flagged as such.
