Lineup Recommender

Which five should start — and which combinations fit? Pick a team and the recommender searches your rotation for the best-fitting units: a balanced starting five plus situational looks, scored on talent, spacing, playmaking, and interior presence.
Pick a team above to build its lineups.
Recommendations optimize a fit score over your rotation (players ranked by your depth chart, with a projected role). Fit rewards talent (projected grade), floor spacing (3+ shooters), a lead ball-handler, and one–two interior anchors — and penalizes a spacing-clogged or point-guard-less five. Situational looks re-weight the same rotation for pace or size. Shooting/assist signals use last season's rates; unproven newcomers are read from position and grade.
