How the boards work. A player's production per minute drifts back toward his own career norm. Bounce-back Overs are players whose last season ran at 85% or less of their earlier per-minute pace; Regression Unders ran at 125% or more. Line is what a book anchored on last season would hang: his last-season per-minute pace × his projected 2026-27 minutes, at the nearest half-point. Tested on the last two seasons, bounce-back points overs went over about 61% of the time, and rebound regression unders went under about 62%. The board header shows the rate for the stat you pick. Cleared it = his past games that went the pick's way at this exact line. Minutes = last season → projected. Value Movers = the biggest gaps between our projection and his career norm. It's a starting point, not a guarantee: always compare with the book's actual number once lines post.
