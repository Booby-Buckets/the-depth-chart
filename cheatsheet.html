Hit rate is level-adjusted. For each past season we set the line at the number he was expected to hit that year — his prior-season average — and measure how often his games beat it. Because that bar moves with his level every season, the rate travels across a transfer: a low-major who kept topping his line stays a strong over even after a jump to a tougher league (his old raw totals never enter the math). Line is our 2026-27 projection — where a book will likely price him. Overs beat their expected line most; Unders least; Value Movers = the biggest gap between our projection and his career norm (the props to watch when lines post). Needs a couple of seasons to normalize, so newcomers appear only on Value Movers. Not a guarantee — a starting point.
