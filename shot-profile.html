Zones are binned from 2025-26 shot-by-shot data (rim = layups/dunks/tips; mid = two-point jumpers; three = all threes). Efficiency is eFG% (threes weighted ×1.5); “expected” is the D-I average make rate for that zone (from the Shot Genome), so a green figure means they finish that zone better than the shot itself is worth. Defense reads shots opponents took against them. Percentages use a large per-team shot sample.
